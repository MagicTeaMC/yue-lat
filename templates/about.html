About

A fast, lightweight URL shortener built with Rust.

Privacy Policy

QuickGo needs no account and uses no analytics or tracking scripts. A cookie protects web forms. Server logs include IP addresses and request details for operations and abuse prevention. The server rotates these logs daily and keeps up to 14 rotated copies.

QuickGo stores short links, their destinations and settings, and counts redirects only for links with a usage limit. Visitor passwords, Edit Tokens and API keys are stored as hashes. Backups can contain link records. Pausing, expiry and revocation stop redirects without deleting records.

Contact the operator with privacy questions.

Terms of Service

Do not use QuickGo for phishing, malware or spam. QuickGo may rate-limit requests and permanently revoke abusive links. A visitor password protects the redirect only. The destination site controls its own access.

QuickGo is provided as is, without warranties. To the extent permitted by law, QuickGo is not liable for losses arising from use or unavailability of the service. QuickGo does not control or endorse linked sites or their content.
